# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Balloon Bursting Game is a fully open-source project built by and for a community of learners, developers and players. We want everyone to feel welcome here, regardless of age, background, gender identity, experience level, nationality, religion, disability or appearance.

We pledge to make participation in this project a friendly, respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

---

## Be Polite and Respectful

Everyone who takes part in the project, in code, issues, pull requests, discussions or any other space, agrees to:

- **Be kind and courteous.** Treat other developers and players the way you would like to be treated.
- **Be patient with beginners.** Many contributors are students or first-time open-source developers. Answer questions helpfully and never make someone feel foolish for asking.
- **Give feedback constructively.** Criticise the code or the idea, never the person. Explain the problem and, where you can, suggest a fix.
- **Accept feedback gracefully.** Reviews exist to improve the project. Do not take them personally.
- **Respect different opinions.** Disagreements are fine. Discuss them calmly and focus on what is best for the project.
- **Give credit.** Acknowledge other people's work and ideas.
- **Communicate clearly.** Write in plain, respectful language. Assume good intent.

### Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal attacks, trolling or name-calling
- Harassment, bullying, intimidation or stalking
- Discriminatory, hateful or offensive language or images
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission
- Spamming, advertising unrelated products or flooding issues and pull requests
- Repeatedly pushing a point after a decision has been made
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional, learning-focused community

## Fair Use of the Project

This project is open source and released under the **MIT License**. You are welcome to use it freely, as long as you follow these fair-use principles:

1. **Keep the licence and credit.** Keep the original copyright notice and licence text in copies or substantial portions of the project.
2. **Do not claim it as solely your own.** You may build on this project, but do not present the original work as entirely yours or misrepresent who created it.
3. **Use it for good.** Do not use the project, its code or its builds to harm others, spread malware, mislead users or break the law.
4. **Do not distribute tampered builds as official.** If you modify the game and share a build, make it clear that it is a modified version and not the official release.
5. **Respect third-party assets.** Some art, sounds or packages may have their own licences. Follow those terms and do not add content to the project unless you have the right to share it.
6. **Do not upload other people's work.** Only contribute code, art, audio or text that you created or that you have permission to share under a compatible licence.
7. **Respect the community.** Do not sell the official download as if it were your own paid product, and do not use the project name to suggest an endorsement that does not exist.
8. **Learn and share.** Using the project for study, assignments, portfolios and personal projects is encouraged. Where it is used for coursework, follow your institution's academic-integrity rules.

## Contribution Etiquette

- Search existing issues and pull requests before opening a new one.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused, with a clear title and description.
- Test your changes in Unity 6.3 LTS before submitting.
- Be responsive. If a reviewer asks for changes, reply or update your pull request when you can. If you no longer have time, say so politely.
- Do not demand quick replies. Maintainers and contributors are volunteers.
- Keep discussions on topic and in the open, so that everyone can learn from them.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel used for the project. It also applies when someone represents the project in public, for example when sharing it on social media or at events.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues and pull requests that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict contributors who engage in behaviour they consider harmful.

Typical steps, depending on how serious the behaviour is:

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1. Reminder | A private or public note explaining what was inappropriate and asking it to stop. |
| 2. Warning | A formal warning with consequences if the behaviour continues. |
| 3. Temporary restriction | A temporary ban from commenting, opening issues or contributing. |
| 4. Permanent removal | A permanent ban from the project for serious or repeated violations. |

Maintainers may skip steps for severe behaviour such as harassment or threats.
